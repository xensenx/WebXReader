## Overview

WebXReader is a local-first reading application designed to help you organize, manage, and read your digital library directly in your browser.

## Features

- **Local-first:** Your library is designed to stay on your device.
- **Multiple formats:** Read supported digital book and document formats.
- **Library management:** Organize books, track reading progress, and manage favorites.
- **Personalized reading:** Customize your reading experience.
- **Privacy-focused:** Read the privacy policy to understand how your data is handled.
