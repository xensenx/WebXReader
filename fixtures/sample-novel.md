# Chapter 1: The Launch Sequence

The countdown was deafening in the cramped observation cockpit. Atmospheric pressure gauges fluttered before settling into green arcs.

> "Navigation computer online. Quantum drives primed for sub-space entry."

Commander Vance gripped the primary thruster manual overrides. Through the viewport, the blue crescent of the home world shrank into a silent sphere.

# Chapter 2: The Void Anomaly

Deep space was neither empty nor cold; it hummed with cosmic radiation and gravitational ripples left behind by dormant supernovas.

Key telemetry points:
- Sector 7G radiation density: 14.2 mSv
- Ion shield dissipation rate: 0.04%
- Sub-light propulsion stability: 99.8%

Suddenly, the sensor array chimed with an unknown frequency.
