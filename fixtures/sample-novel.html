Chapter 1: The Scholar's Gambit

The library had stood for a thousand years, guarding scrolls penned in dialects long dead. Julian blew a layer of white dust off the vellum.

"Knowledge is neither a weapon nor a shield," Master Corvin had once remarked, "it is merely the lantern that shows where the precipice begins."

Chapter 2: The Midnight Conclave

Seven brass lanterns were lit around the octagonal table. Outside, rain lashed against the leaded panes with relentless fury.

None of the elders spoke until the bells in the lower district tolled twelve times.
